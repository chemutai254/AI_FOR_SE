# Theoretical Analysis
## Q1: Explain how Edge AI reduces latency and enhances privacy compared to cloud-based AI. Provide a real-world example (e.g., autonomous drones).
- Edge AI’s latency is low due to its ability to process data locally, real time processing, enhanced privacy and security of data. Cloud-based AI on the other hand, experiences high latency due to its dependency on network to process data. The use of network increases the security risk of data since data has to be processed from the servers. 
- For example, the use of IoT devices in a greenhouse to monitor temperature and humidity of data utilizes edge AI, where the data is used to make critical decisions for farmers. A cloud-based AI can be used in retail industry to process customer data and provide predictive analytics, streamlining business processes.

## Q2: Compare Quantum AI and classical AI in solving optimization problems. What industries could benefit most from Quantum AI?
- Quantum AI uses quantum computing resources based on quantum bits or qubits and can exist in multiple states. Quantum AI is known for processing vast amount of data in parallel, enabling it to be solve complex challenges such as optimization efficiently compared to Classical AI. Classical AI utilizes traditional computing resources processing data in binary bits represented as 0, and 1, thus unable to handle complex and optimization challenges.
- Quantum AI is used to train deep neural networks or analyze high dimensional data such as image processing and natural language processing. It can be utilized in financial modeling to generate optimal strategies through optimization. Classical AI is used in traditional ML, thus, used in solving repetitive tasks and handling real-world challenges.
